Privacy Policy for KIKI TIMER

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Definitions

  • Developer: Refers to the individual or entity operating under the publisher name "Knowledgebread" on the Google Play Store, who is the creator, owner, and maintainer of the "KIKI TIMER" application.
  • User: Refers to the individual downloading, installing, or using the "KIKI TIMER" application.
  • App: Refers to the "KIKI TIMER" mobile application.

2. Introduction

The Developer respects the privacy of the User. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when the User accesses and uses the App.

3. Data Collection and Usage

The App is designed to function without requiring user registration.

  • No Account Required: The Developer does not require, request, or collect any personal identification information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. There is no user login system.
  • Local Data Storage (Workout Logs & Timers): The App allows the User to create custom timers and generates workout logs. The Developer does not collect, transmit, or store these workout logs or custom timers on any external servers. All timer configurations, audio preferences, and activity logs are stored entirely locally on the User's device.
  • Export Feature: The App provides a feature for the User to export their own workout logs to their device's storage. The handling and sharing of these exported files are entirely at the discretion and responsibility of the User.

4. Third-Party Services

While the Developer does not directly collect personal data, the App utilizes integrated third-party services that may collect data to function properly:

  • Firebase Analytics: The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used, track crashes, and identify performance issues. Firebase may collect anonymous usage data, device models, operating system versions, and generalized geographical locations. This data helps the Developer improve the App's stability and features.
  • AppLovin (Advertisements): The App uses the AppLovin MAX SDK to display advertisements. AppLovin and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, and generalized location data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. Where required by regional laws (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA in the United States), the App will present a consent dialogue allowing the User to manage their preferences regarding personalized advertising.

The User is encouraged to review the privacy policies of these third-party service providers:

  • Google/Firebase Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

5. Security

The Developer values the User's trust in using the App. Because workout and timer data are kept locally on the device, the security of this data relies on the security of the User's device. The User is encouraged to use standard device security measures (like screen locks and encryption) to protect their local data.

6. Children’s Privacy

In accordance with our Terms of Use, the App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18 without parental consent. The Developer does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The User is advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
